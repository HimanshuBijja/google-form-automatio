AutoApply - Field Customizer & Settings

Customize your questions, answers, and question types for automatic Google Form filling.

💡 How it works: AutoApply scans each question in the Google Form. If the question matches your Question, it applies the Type (types text/email/phone, clicks the matching radio button, or checks the checkbox) using your Answer.
